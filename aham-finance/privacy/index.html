Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Aham Finance is a desktop application developed by Ragith Sabapathipillai, operating as Ragith Software Consulting. It helps construction businesses organize Google Drive receipt expenses and Connecteam labor by project.

In brief: your imported workspace is stored on your Mac. The app connects directly to Google and Connecteam, uses read-only access, and does not automatically send your receipts, labor information, or project finances to the developer or an AI service.

Information the app accesses

Google account and Drive

When you connect Google Drive, the app reads your account email address and Google account identifier to identify the connected account. It receives authorization tokens so it can reconnect and refresh data without asking you to sign in each time, while that authorization remains valid.

Aham Finance requests the Google Drive read-only permission, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly. This permission technically permits reading and downloading the Drive files your account can access; it is not restricted by Google to folders selected in the app. The current app uses it to browse or search folder metadata and scan receipt-file metadata in the folders you select and their subfolders.

Metadata includes file and folder names, identifiers, folder relationships, file types, modification information, and Drive links. Receipt filenames can themselves contain project or customer names, amounts, and expense descriptions. The app extracts these values from filenames. It does not download PDF or image contents during receipt import, perform OCR, or create, edit, move, or delete Drive files. Opening a receipt in Drive uses your browser and Google’s service.

Connecteam

When you provide a Connecteam API key, the app reads account/company identifiers, projects and time clocks, worker identifiers and names, and timesheet data for the requested period. This includes work dates, project references, recorded time, available pay items and applied rates, calculated pay, and approval/submission status.

Connecteam responses can contain additional fields. The app retains the normalized data needed for project labor calculations; it does not retain employee phone numbers, email addresses, or punch-location coordinates in its imported workspace. Its integration does not write or modify Connecteam records.

Information you enter

The app stores your folder choices, receipt-to-project assignments, local project names, category corrections, review decisions, optional CAD contract values, and import/sync settings.

How information is used

  • Discover receipts in selected folders and extract names, amounts, and expense reasons from filenames.
  • Suggest and remember receipt-to-project associations, including choices you confirm.
  • Show project receipt expenses, available labor hours/pay, recorded expenditure, and optional contract comparisons.
  • Identify incomplete or ambiguous records so you can review them.
  • Restore your saved workspace and refresh connected sources while the app is running.

Imported Google and Connecteam information is not used for advertising, sold, or used to train general-purpose AI models. The current app does not send imported information to an AI service.

Use and transfer of Google API data follow the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Local storage and security

Imported metadata, normalized labor records, and your local choices are stored in a database on your Mac. Google authorization tokens and the Connecteam API key are stored in macOS Keychain. The app does not ask for or store your Google password.

Connections to Google and Connecteam use HTTPS. Local files use operating-system access permissions. The workspace database is not separately encrypted by the app; protection also depends on your Mac account, device security, and any disk encryption or backups you use.

The app does not automatically upload your workspace to a developer-hosted server or send automatic analytics or crash reports. People who can access your Mac account or its backups may be able to access locally saved information. Backups and copies you create are controlled separately.

Sharing and third-party services

The app communicates with Google and Connecteam to authenticate and retrieve the information needed for its features. Those providers process requests under their own policies: Google’s Privacy Policy and Connecteam’s Privacy Policy.

The developer does not automatically receive imported project, receipt, or worker information. If you choose to send screenshots, files, or other information when requesting support, that information is used to investigate and respond to your request. Do not send API keys, authorization tokens, or unnecessary personal or financial information.

Retention

Your saved workspace remains on your Mac until you remove it. Successful refreshes update the current imported records, but historical labor records and local review choices can remain saved. Closing or uninstalling the app does not necessarily remove its database, Keychain entries, test profiles, or backups. Revoking access stops future authorized retrieval but does not erase previously imported local data.

Your controls and data removal

  • Choose what to import: select the receipt folders and labor date range in the app. The underlying Google read-only permission remains broader than the selected folders.
  • Stop background refreshes: quit the app. It does not sync while closed.
  • Revoke Google access: visit your Google Account’s third-party connections and remove Aham Finance, or the app name shown during authorization.
  • Revoke Connecteam access: revoke the API key in your Connecteam account’s API settings. A key used by other integrations will stop working there too.
  • Remove locally saved information: quit the app, then remove its workspace and saved Keychain entries. The packaged app’s default workspace is ~/Library/Application Support/AhamFinance; its Keychain service is AhamFinanceDesktopV2. Removing these erases locally saved decisions and credentials. Separate test profiles, exports, and backups must be removed separately. Contact the developer if you need help locating the right files.

Manage the original receipts, worker records, and other source information directly in Google Drive or Connecteam. Deleting the local workspace does not delete those source records.

This website and support enquiries

The Aham Finance homepage and this policy are static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. These pages add no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies, or embedded third-party resources. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security; see GitHub Pages’ data collection information and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

If you use the Contact form on the developer’s portfolio website, the name, email address, and message you submit are processed through its third-party form provider and delivered to the developer for handling your enquiry. That form is separate from the desktop app. For questions about correspondence or information you have shared directly, contact the developer using the same channel.
